About We Love Rishikesh

A community-driven movement to protect, sustain, and celebrate the soul of Rishikesh

“Rishikesh belongs to everyone. Our job is to make sure it stays that way.”

The We Love Rishikesh initiative is a digitally-integrated urban intervention — a mandatory E-permit system that serves as a legal travel ticket, safety insurance, and a cultural identity badge. Every rupee collected from the levy is reinvested directly into the city's infrastructure, ecology, and community.

Three Pillars

How the System Works

Protect

Safeguarding Rishikesh's fragile ecology, sacred ghats, and mountain geology from the pressure of mass tourism.

Sustain

Every levy collected funds EV parking, cleaner ghats, safer ferries, and better infrastructure for residents and visitors alike.

Celebrate

Promoting local culture, vendors, events, and traditions so that tourism strengthens — not erodes — the city's identity.
